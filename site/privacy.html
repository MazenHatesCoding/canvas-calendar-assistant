Privacy policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Canvas Calendar Assistant ("the tool") is self-hosted software. Each person who uses it runs their own copy with their own accounts and keys. The developer does not operate a server for it and does not collect, receive or store any user's data.

What the tool accesses

  • Canvas: your course list, course announcements and inbox messages, read with an access token you create. The tool only reads from Canvas.
  • Google Calendar: a calendar the tool creates in your account, and the events it adds to that calendar.
  • Your timetable: the class schedule you write down for the tool.
  • Telegram: the messages you send to your own notification bot.

How Google user data is used

The tool uses its Google Calendar access only to create its own calendar in your account and to create, update and delete events in that calendar. It does not read your other calendars or events.

Google user data is not sold, not used for advertising, not used to train AI models, and not transferred to anyone, including the developer. The tool's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Where your data goes

  • Google Gemini API: the text of each new announcement or message, together with your timetable, is sent to Google's Gemini API using your own API key, to work out dates and details. Google's terms for that API apply to this text.
  • Google Calendar: the resulting events are saved in your Google account.
  • Telegram: short notifications about those events are sent to you through your own Telegram bot.
  • GitHub: if you run the tool on GitHub Actions, it runs in your own GitHub account and your keys are kept in that account's encrypted secrets.

Nothing is sent anywhere else.

Storage and deletion

The tool keeps no database of its own. Your events live in your Google Calendar, and your keys live on your computer or in your GitHub account. To remove everything, delete the calendar the tool created, revoke its access at myaccount.google.com/permissions, delete your Canvas access token, and delete your copy of the tool.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
