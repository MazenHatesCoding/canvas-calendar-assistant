Privacy policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Canvas Calendar Assistant ("the tool") is self-hosted software. Each person who uses it runs their own copy with their own accounts and keys. The developer does not collect or store any user's data. The only thing the developer's website does for you is the one-time Google sign-in described below.

What the tool accesses

  • Canvas: your course list, course announcements and inbox messages, read with an access token you create. The tool only reads from Canvas.
  • Google Calendar: a calendar the tool creates in your account, and the events it adds to that calendar.
  • Your timetable: the class schedule you write down for the tool.
  • Telegram: the messages you send to your own notification bot.

How Google user data is used

The tool uses its Google Calendar access only to create its own calendar in your account and to create, update and delete events in that calendar. It does not read your other calendars or events.

Google user data is not sold, not used for advertising, not used to train AI models, and not transferred to anyone. The developer does not keep any of it. The tool's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

The one-time Google sign-in

To connect Google Calendar you use the connect page on this website once. It sends you to Google to sign in, receives the one-time code Google issues, exchanges it with Google for the access your copy needs, creates the tool's calendar in your account, and shows the result to you in your browser so you can save it in your own copy. The website does not store or log the code, the resulting access, or anything about your Google account, and it has no access to your account afterwards. From then on only your own copy talks to Google.

Where your data goes

  • Google Gemini API: the text of each new announcement or message, together with your timetable, is sent to Google's Gemini API using your own API key, to work out dates and details. Google's terms for that API apply to this text.
  • Google Calendar: the resulting events are saved in your Google account.
  • Telegram: short notifications about those events are sent to you through your own Telegram bot.
  • Vercel: if you host the tool on Vercel, it runs in your own Vercel account and your keys are kept in that account's environment variables. Your Canvas posts and Telegram messages pass through your copy there while it works; it does not store them.

Nothing is sent anywhere else.

Storage and deletion

The tool keeps no database of its own. Your events live in your Google Calendar, and your keys live on your computer or in your Vercel account. To remove everything, delete the calendar the tool created, revoke its access at myaccount.google.com/permissions, delete your Canvas access token, and delete your copy of the tool.
