Canvas Calendar Assistant
Privacy policy Terms

Your course announcements, turned into calendar events.

Canvas Calendar Assistant is a small tool a student runs for themselves. It reads the announcements their instructors post on Canvas and adds the quizzes, deadlines and schedule changes to their own Google Calendar.

What it does

  1. Checks your Canvas courses for new announcements and messages once an hour.
  2. Works out which ones are things you have to do or attend, and when, using your own class timetable.
  3. Creates or updates events in a dedicated calendar in your Google Calendar account.
  4. Sends you a Telegram message about what was added.

How it uses Google Calendar

The tool asks for access to Google Calendar for one purpose: to create a calendar of its own in your account and to add, change and remove the events it puts there. It does not read, change or share your other calendars or events.

Who runs it

There is no central service. Each student runs their own copy with their own accounts and keys, so their data stays in accounts they control. The developer of this tool never receives it.

Details are in the privacy policy and the terms of use.
