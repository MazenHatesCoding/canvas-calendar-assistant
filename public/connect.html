Connect your Google Calendar

This is one step of setting up your own copy. It gives the assistant a calendar of its own in your Google account, and gives you two values to paste into Vercel.

1. Sign in with Google

The button opens Google in a new tab. Three things will look odd there. All three are expected:

  1. "Google hasn't verified this app." This is a student project, not a company. Choose Advanced, then Go to Canvas to Google Calendar Assistant.
  2. A permission box. Leave it ticked. It lets the assistant manage only the calendar it creates, never your other calendars.
  3. An error page at the end, saying the site can't be reached. That is the finish line. Copy the whole address from the top of that page, it starts with http://localhost.

2. Paste the address you copied

Working, this takes a few seconds…

3. Your two values

A calendar named "University" now exists in your Google Calendar. Keep this page open and paste these into Vercel when the setup guide asks for them. Treat both like passwords.

CRON_SECRET is a random password made in your browser just now. It keeps strangers from starting your copy's Canvas check, and you will need it once more on your own setup page.

This page passes your sign-in to Google and hands the result straight back to you. Nothing is kept. Details are in the privacy policy.
